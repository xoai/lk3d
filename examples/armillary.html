An armillary sphere

A model of the sky made of rings, set for Hà Nội's latitude (21° N). Go through the steps under it, or play the sky's daily turn: the cage of celestial circles turns about the polar axis, east to west, round a still Earth. The scene also says what must be true of it (expect): the Earth inside the turning cage, the bearings on the axis, the meridian's size; lk3d check proves each.
