A tour of a human cell

An illustrated cell, cut open, gone through in eleven guided steps: its membrane and the proteins in it, the nucleus with its pores and loose chromatin, rough and smooth endoplasmic reticulum, the Golgi apparatus, mitochondria, lysosomes, the three fibres of the cytoskeleton and the centrosome. Messenger RNA leaves the nucleus through its pores, and the membrane has a close-up of its own (a second figure). The scene is made by cell-tour.mjs from the cell of the animal cell.
