Air through a ducted fan

Streaks of air drawn with flow(): cool air round the core, and air through the core that leaves hot (a colour ramp along each path). The throttle sets how many streaks are drawn (amount) and how fast they run (speed); slow motion is set({ timeScale }). Try the pen look and the themes: on a light page the streaks are inked, on a dark one they glow.

An illustration: the streaks show where air goes and roughly how fast, not a simulation.
