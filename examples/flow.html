Air through a ducted fan

Air as moving volumes drawn with stream(): cool air drawn into the intake, the bypass air through the duct round the core (a stream with an inner and an outer radius), and the jet that leaves the nozzle hot (a colour ramp along it). Each speeds up where it narrows and slows where it widens. The throttle sets how fast they run (speed) and how thick they are (density); slow motion is set({ timeScale }). Try the looks and the themes.

An illustration: the streams show where air goes and roughly how fast, not a simulation.
