An AC generator

Point at a part to read about it. Take the machine apart, cut it open, make the casing see-through. Drag the rotor (the magnets or the shaft) round to turn it by hand: the lamp lights with the speed of your hand. The page can keep what you have done as a link (fig.state() and LK3D.encodeState), and lock what you may change (fig.lock).
