An AC generator

Point at a part to read about it; click one for its card over the figure (card: true), where the rotor and the lamp show values the page works out as they change (fig.card), as their labels do under their names (fig.tagText). Take the machine apart, cut it open, make the casing see-through. Drag the rotor (the magnets or the shaft) round to turn it by hand: the lamp lights with the speed of your hand. The page can keep what you have done as a link (fig.state() and LK3D.encodeState), and lock what you may change (fig.lock).
