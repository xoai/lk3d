lk3d examples

Each page loads ../dist/lk3d.min.js (run npm run build) and is meant to be read: open its source. Serve this folder with npm run examples.

  • An AC generator: take it apart, cut it open, see through the casing, and spin the rotor by hand (a turn handle).
  • A converging lens: drag the object and the focal points; rays drawn with lines meet at the image.
  • An animal cell: a cut-open cell with a true scale bar; choose an organelle and the camera frames it.
  • Molecules: ball-and-stick and space-filling molecules in CPK colours, to one scale.
  • The inner Solar System: drag a planet along its orbit (an along handle), in the night environment.
  • Rock layers: a block of ground with tilted layers and a fault, from the terrain and extrude shapes.
  • A pressure gauge: a needle that turns between stops, read out for screen readers.
  • A fossil skull: a textured scan (glTF) of a 1.8-million-year-old Homo erectus skull, CC0 from the Smithsonian.
  • Two looks: the same model in the drawn look and the realistic studio look, side by side.
  • Current in a circuit: particles flowing along a closed path, faster as the voltage rises, and a glowing filament.
