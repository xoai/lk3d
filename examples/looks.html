Three looks, and themes

The same scene in the drawn look (style: 'ink': coloured-pencil bands, hatching, ink outlines), the studio look ('studio': physically based materials, a generated environment, soft shadows) and the pen look ('pen': near-white solids in a few close tones, thin lines, colour only for accents). Readers go round them with the built-in button; a page sets the first.
