Three looks, and themes

The same scene in the pen look (style: 'pen', the default: light solids, thin outlines and edges, a soft shadow, full colour for accents and a soft wash of it elsewhere), the studio look ('studio': physically based materials, a generated environment, soft shadows) and the drawn look ('ink': coloured-pencil bands, hatching, ink outlines). Readers go round them with the built-in button; a page sets the first.
