A fossil skull

A textured glTF model: the Smithsonian's scan of KNM-ER 3733, a Homo erectus skull about 1.8 million years old (CC0). It is in millimetres, so the 5 cm bar is true. lk3d decodes the colour and normal maps in the background and draws the bone's base colour until they arrive. The model's credit and its source (scan) appear under the figure.
