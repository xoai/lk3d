A desk fan taken apart

A sequence takes the fan apart one part at a time, the camera on each part as it leaves; played back, it puts the fan together. Spin and oscillate are one choice: playing one stops the other. Press Play the steps under the figure and the steps play by themselves; the page writes its own caption for each step and stage. Touch the figure, a key or a control and the tour pauses; Play goes on from there.

The fan is drawn to no scale. The scene is scenes/teardown.json; the sequence's stages give each part its way out (its own explode vector, or by) and a view for the camera.
