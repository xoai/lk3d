# Anti-patterns

Each of these is a mistake made while building real lk3d figures, and how it is avoided now.

- **A lone object in empty space.** The first jet engine was a nacelle floating in the middle of the stage, with
  19 parts and its inside hidden until the reader cut it open. It read as a toy. Put the object where it works (a
  stand, a base plate, a bench), show the inside by default when the inside is the idea (`section`, a cut-away), and
  build it from enough parts to be believed (30–60).
- **One big plain body.** A casing drawn as a single smooth shell with nothing on it. Break large surfaces with
  structure: flanges, seams, bolt circles, ribs, access panels, a label plate.
- **A casing that leaves through what is inside it.** The jet engine's casings lifted straight up when taken apart,
  sweeping through the rotors and shafts on the way. `lk3d check` now reports it ("run into each other when taken
  apart"): send a casing out along the axis, or split it in two halves that move apart sideways.
- **A part sent out through its neighbour.** A neuron's nucleus lifted straight up through a dendrite. The same check
  caught it; the fix was a path that clears the branches (up and towards the reader).
- **Two pictures of one thing shown at once.** A ball-and-stick molecule and its space-filling twin in the same
  place: the first was invisible inside the second, with its label pointing into it. Show one at a time
  (`set({hide})`) and say so in `check.allow`.
- **A sign error in a rotation.** A gauge's back plate was turned the wrong way and covered its own dial. Render
  every look and pose and look at each picture; a check of numbers does not see it.
- **Particles inside a wire.** Dots of current ran along the centre of a wire, so the wire hid them. Run particles and
  flow streaks just outside a solid, or make the solid see-through (`xray`, `opacity`).
- **Glass that hides a handle.** A handle behind a glass casing could not be grabbed or seen. Handles go where the
  reader can reach them; a handle is picked through glass, but the reader must still see it.
- **Labels stuck in a corner.** Labels drawn before the first picture sat at the top left until something moved. The
  engine now places them with the first picture; if a label still points at the wrong place, give the part a
  `label: [x, y, z]` on its visible surface.
- **A measuring tool hidden by what it measures.** A caliper on a fossil skull disappeared behind the skull in the side
  view. Place tools in front of the object from every preset view, or move them with the view.
- **A made-up model shown as real.** A model generated by AI was nearly shown as a scan of a specimen. Every model says
  where it comes from (`source: scan | dataset | artist | ai`), and an AI model is called an illustration.
- **Stripes on a curved surface.** The first pen look shaded every surface in four hard steps, so a round casing
  showed contour bands. Now flat faces take one tone each and curves shade smoothly; if a curved part still looks
  faceted, raise its `detail`.
- **Colour everywhere.** Every part in its own saturated colour leaves nothing for the eye to find. Grey structure,
  a few group colours, one accent.
- **Silencing a check.** `check.allow` and `check.float` excuse what the scene means (planets float; two models of one
  molecule take turns), never a collision you did not want to fix. Fix the geometry.
