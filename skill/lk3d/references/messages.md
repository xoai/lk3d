# What the checks say, and what to do

`lk3d check` first checks the format (every field, every shape, the scene's own references), then the geometry.
Format problems come with the path of the field (`parts[3].shapes[1].box`); fix them first, since the geometry is
only checked on a scene in the right form. **A misspelt field on a part is not reported** (a page may keep its own
data there): `colour` for `color`, `explod` for `explode` are silently ignored, so look at the pictures. A misspelt
field of a shape, a motion, a joint, a vessel, a liquid, a gas or `check` is reported.

## Format (lk3d/validate)

| It says                                                          | It means                                                                                  | Fix                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `a shape is exactly one of: lathe, cylinder, …`                  | the shape has no kind, two kinds, or an unknown one                                       | one kind per shape (`{ "box": … }`, `{ "lathe": … }`); the kinds are in the shapes guide |
| `three numbers [x, y, z]` on `box`                               | a box needs three sizes                                                                   | `"box": [x, y, z]`                                                                       |
| `moves "X", which is no part or group`                           | a handle carries a part that does not exist                                               | the id or group of a real part                                                           |
| `"X" is used by two parts`                                       | two parts with one id                                                                     | ids are unique; a shared name goes in `group`                                            |
| `model "X" is not under the scene's models`                      | a `model` shape names a model the scene does not list                                     | add it under `models` with its file, licence, author and source                          |
| `should be 1` on `lk3d`                                          | the scene says a format version this engine does not read                                 | `"lk3d": 1`, or a newer lk3d                                                             |
| `is not a field here`                                            | a field this object does not take (a misspelling, or a field of another kind of shape)    | the field as the scene format names it ([scene format](../../../docs/scene-format.md))   |
| `needs shapes (or is a liquid or a gas)`                         | a part with no `shapes` that no liquid or gas draws                                       | give it shapes, or name it as a liquid's or a gas's `part`                               |
| `"X" is no part` / `"X" is no vessel`                            | a vessel, liquid or gas (its `part`, `in` or `piston`) names what the scene does not have | the id of a real part, or of a vessel under `vessels`                                    |
| `"X" is no other liquid` on `into`                               | a liquid pours into a liquid that does not exist, or into itself                          | the `part` of the liquid in the vessel below                                             |
| `"X" is no motion` on `outlet.valve` or `drive`                  | a valve or a joint's drive names a motion the scene does not have                         | the id of a motion under `motions`                                                       |
| `heights go up from the bottom to the brim`                      | a vessel's `inside` is not in order of height                                             | `[height, radius]` pairs from the bottom up                                              |
| `"X" is used by two motions` / `"X" is used by two joints`       | two motions, or two joints, with one id                                                   | ids are unique among motions, and among joints                                           |
| `runs from a value to a larger one`                              | a motion's `range` is `[a, b]` with b not above a                                         | `"range": [0, 360]`                                                                      |
| `keys go in order of their values, within the range [a, b]`      | a track's keys are out of order or outside the motion's range                             | keys sorted by their value, each within `range`                                          |
| `moves "X", which is no part or group` on a joint or a track     | a joint or a motion's track moves a part that does not exist                              | the id or group of a real part                                                           |
| `"X" is on joint "Y" already`                                    | one part on two joints                                                                    | a part on one joint; carry it with another by `parent`                                   |
| `"X" is no joint`                                                | `parent`, `follow.joint`, `rod.joint` or `aim.joint` names a joint that does not exist    | the id of a joint under `joints`                                                         |
| `a joint reads another joint, not itself`                        | a joint names itself as its parent, or follows, rides or aims at itself                   | another joint's id                                                                       |
| `a rod holds a slide (a piston): give the joint slide, not turn` | `rod` on a joint that turns                                                               | `slide: { axis }` for the piston; the rod's own swing is an `aim`                        |
| `an aim turns its parts: give the joint turn, not slide`         | `aim` on a joint that slides                                                              | `turn: { axis, center }`                                                                 |
| `its value comes from one place: drive or follow` (or rod, aim)  | a joint with two of `drive`, `follow`, `rod`, `aim`                                       | keep one                                                                                 |
| `"X" reads itself through other joints (a loop)`                 | joints that follow, ride on or aim at each other in a ring                                | break the ring: one of them takes its value from a motion (`drive`)                      |

## Geometry (lk3d/lint)

| It says                                                                                                                         | It means                                                                                                             | Fix                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `A and B run into each other when taken apart (… % of one inside the other, … % at rest)`                                       | on the way to their `explode` places, one passes through the other: the reader sees parts sliding through each other | give one of them a path that is clear (along the axis, sideways, towards the reader), or split a casing in halves; only when it is meant (two pictures of one thing, shown in turn), `check: {allow: [[A, B]]}`              |
| `A touches no other part and hangs in the air`                                                                                  | its bounds touch no part and it is above the lowest part                                                             | rest it on something (a bracket, a post, the base); a part meant to float (a planet, a molecule beside another, a cell across a gap) goes under `check: {float: [A]}`, or `float: true` for a whole scene of floating things |
| `the label of A points inside B when taken apart`                                                                               | the label's leader line ends inside another solid part, where no one sees it                                         | `"label": [x, y, z]` on A's visible surface; or B see-through (`xray`, `opacity`) if it should be                                                                                                                            |
| `A makes no triangles: check its shapes (an empty outline, a radius of 0, a node the model lacks)`                              | the part draws nothing: the rest of the check cannot see it                                                          | fix the shape's numbers, or the `node` name in the model                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `A has a shape whose size or place is not a number: check its fields`                                                           | a shape's numbers give NaN (a size or a place that is not a number, a division by 0)                                 | numbers in every size, `at`, `rot` and `scale`                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `A and B run into each other as M moves (… % of one inside the other, … % at its start): change the motion's keys or joints`    | on the way through motion M's range, one part passes through the other                                               | change the keys or joints so they clear each other; only when it is meant, `check: {allow: [[A, B]]}`                                                                                                                        |
| `V holds … but its inside takes …: less liquid, or a larger inside`                                                             | the liquids in vessel V add up to more than its inside holds                                                         | a smaller `volume`, or a wider or taller `inside`                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `V would overflow if all that pours into it came (…; it takes …)`                                                               | what is in V and what can pour into it from above is more than V holds                                               | less liquid above, or a larger vessel below                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `the inside given for V goes through the walls of P (… % of one in the other): give its radius at each height inside the glass` | the `inside` profile is wider than the vessel's own part (or the glass sits inside the liquid)                       | radii a little inside the glass at each height                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `the gas in V would push its piston out of it: fewer moles, a lower temperature, or a taller vessel`                            | by pV = nRT, the piston would rest above the top of V's `inside`                                                     | fewer moles `n`, a lower `T`, a heavier piston (`mass`), or a taller `inside`                                                                                                                                                |
| `the model X: no file …`                                                                                                        | a model's file is not beside the scene, the page or the working folder                                               | the right `file`, or the model's `data` (base64)                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Render

| It says                        | It means                                                     | Fix                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `lk3d render needs Playwright` | no headless browser to draw with                             | `npm i -D playwright && npx playwright install chromium`; if you cannot, say the figure was not checked by eye |
| `errors: …` after a picture    | the page threw while drawing                                 | read the message: usually a shape's numbers (a path of one point, a radius of 0)                               |
| a picture with nothing in it   | the camera is not on the model, or the model is tiny or huge | `view: {target, dist}`; keep the model about 1–10 units across                                                 |
